Almost every 3D object starts as a simple shape. Learn four, and you can build nearly anything.

## The four shapes
Drag the scene to look around.

```3d
cube: red, label Cube
sphere: blue, label Sphere
cone: yellow, label Cone
cylinder: green, label Cylinder
caption: The four basic shapes
labels: on
```

- A **cube** has six square faces.
- A **sphere** is round from every side.
- A **cone** narrows to a point.
- A **cylinder** has two round ends.
